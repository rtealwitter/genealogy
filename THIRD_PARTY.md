# Sources and third-party software

Advisor relationships and degree metadata are attributed to the Mathematics Genealogy Project and the individual faculty sources linked in each record. CMC supplies the default faculty roster. This project is independent of those organizations and does not claim ownership of their source material. The MIT license applies to this project's code, not an assertion of rights over source data.

Locally bundled libraries retain their license notices in `vendor/`:

- Dagre and Graphlib: MIT.
- jsPDF: MIT; bundled dependencies retain their notices.
- DejaVu fonts: Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license, reproduced in `assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt`.

The interface uses DM Sans and Libre Caslon Display from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License), with system-font fallbacks. Poster files use embedded DejaVu fonts and do not require a font service.
