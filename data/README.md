# The CMC snapshot

`genealogy.json` is the complete reachable snapshot: **543 people and 661 academic mentor links**, with no pending records. This includes 13 direct doctoral students of the selected faculty and their co-advisor ancestry; the selection itself remains the 12 faculty. See [the student extension audit](STUDENTS-AUDIT.md) for the additions and coverage limits. `cmc-roots.json` is its reproducible seed. See [the audit](AUDIT.md) for verification, shared-ancestor counts, and source limitations. The starting group is the 12 people listed in the regular faculty section of [CMC's Mathematical Sciences faculty page](https://www.cmc.edu/mathematics/math-faculty), before the visiting and emeritus sections, checked September 25, 2026. This interprets the requested group as **tenured and tenure-track** faculty, rather than only untenured assistant professors. The department page does not label each person's individual tenure status.

Eleven people have identifiable Mathematics Genealogy Project records. Mike Izbicki's 2017 UC Riverside doctorate and advisor Christian R. Shelton are documented in his [faculty CV](https://www.cmc.edu/sites/default/files/2024-06/IzbickiM2023CV.pdf); no matching MGP student record was found in the surname search. His advisor's MGP record continues that branch. R. Teal Witter appears in MGP as Raylen Teal Witter; the year, institution, and both advisors agree with [his CV](https://www.rtealwitter.com/cv/cv.pdf).

Every student record links to the sources for its advisor relationships. MGP often records multiple advisors, and all listed advisor links are retained. A single displayed degree year/institution uses MGP's first degree entry. Historical relationships in MGP can predate the modern doctorate and should be read as academic mentorship, not necessarily PhD supervision. Explicit tutor, teacher, mentor, or supervisor labels are retained and identified in the person’s note. A person with no recorded advisor is a limit of the source, not proof that the person had no advisor. Incomplete fetches are marked explicitly.

Names, institutions, years, and relationships are factual source data. This independent project does not claim ownership of the underlying MGP database, and its code license does not relicense that database. Please cite and support the [Mathematics Genealogy Project](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/).
